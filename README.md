# globalsoilmuseums-net.github.io

The website brings together the global network of soil museums and their collections.
It connects museums, researchers, educators, artists, and communities interested in soils.
Here, you can discover exhibitions, collections, projects, resources, and stories from around the world.
The repository highlights the remarkable diversity of ways in which soils are studied, shared, and understood.
It makes knowledge and experiences from soil museums more visible and accessible.
It also supports exchange and collaboration across countries, disciplines, and cultures.
From scientific collections to creative interpretations, each contribution reveals a different dimension of soil.
Together, these resources show why soils matter for life, landscapes, food, climate, and society.
Explore the network, discover its museums, and connect with people working to make soils visible.
